How to read them

  • Every artifact has guided chapters in the header. P plays them as a story.
  • / finds a node by name, R traces an exact route, and L compares two roles.
  • M opens the overview radar, F enters the presentation stage, and Esc leaves it.
  • E exports a PNG, an SVG, a video, or a share card. Exports are always the full diagram.
  • Architecture nodes marked SRC n open the exact file and line range, pinned to one commit.

Regenerating

  • visual/sources/*.json is the editable source of truth for every diagram.
  • zsh visual/check.sh validates all of them at showcase quality.
  • zsh visual/deliver.sh re-renders and commits the artifacts.
  • zsh visual/verify.sh collects bounded desktop browser evidence.
  • node visual/build-site.mjs regenerates this index and the diagram pages.

Generated from the Catalyst repository with Archify. Every diagram is a validated, self-contained artifact with a typed JSON source.

Each artifact supports ? for its guide, / to find a node, P to play the guided chapters, F for the presentation stage, and S / T to change visual style or theme.
